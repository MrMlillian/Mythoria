Our Mission

Storybook is a small library of classic short stories — one chapter each — built for readers who love atmosphere, imagination, and the feeling of turning a page. Every book can be read in a single sitting.

The Collection

Thirty self-contained stories spanning fairy tales, fables, fantasy, horror, romance, mystery, thriller, comedy, drama, and adventure. Start anywhere and read at your own pace.

Where the Stories Come From

All stories are in the public domain and are free to read and share:

  • Ten tales by the Brothers Grimm, from Grimm’s Fairy Tales (edited by Frances Jenkins Olcott, Project Gutenberg #52521).
  • Twenty fables attributed to Aesop, from Three Hundred Æsop’s Fables (translated by George Fyler Townsend, Project Gutenberg #21).

Reader Features

  • Dark and light reading modes, remembered on every page.
  • A like button on each story.
  • Show or hide the synopsis.
  • A reading-progress bar while you scroll.
  • Search and genre filters in the Library.

The Project

This site was created as a final project for ITE 1. It combines semantic HTML, one custom CSS file with Flexbox and Grid, and one JavaScript file for the interactive features.
