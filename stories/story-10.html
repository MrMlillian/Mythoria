The Cartographer's Daughter

Author: Isolde Fairchild

Genre: Historical

Synopsis

A girl secretly finishes her father's map and discovers a coastline no one else believes exists.

Chapter 1: Ink and Salt

In the year 1604, Maren's father went blind, but the harbor merchants still waited for his maps. Maren copied his strokes in secret, line by careful line.

One night she added a coastline from a sailor's drunken tale and signed it with her father's mark.

Months later a ship returned with salt-stained men who swore they had landed there, and who asked, trembling, to meet the mapmaker.
