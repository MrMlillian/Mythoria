The Last Bus to Nowhere

Author: Priya Nandakumar

Genre: Mystery

Synopsis

A night bus driver notices the same passenger boarding at a stop that doesn't exist.

Chapter 1: Stop 13

Every Thursday at 11:48 p.m., the number 9 bus stopped at a corner where there was no sign, no shelter, and no road. And every Thursday a woman in a green coat stepped on, paid exact fare, and sat in the back.

Driver Halloran had never dared ask her anything. Tonight, rain hammering the windshield, he finally glanced in the mirror and found the seat empty.

On it lay a ticket stamped with tomorrow's date and a single line: 'You are the one who stops.'
