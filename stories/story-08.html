The Fox Who Borrowed Thunder

Author: Samir Haddad

Genre: Fable

Synopsis

A clever fox tricks the storm into lending him its voice, and learns the cost.

Chapter 1: A Very Loud Fox

Once there was a fox who was tired of being small. 'If only I had a voice like thunder,' he sighed, 'the forest would listen.'

The storm heard him and laughed. 'Take it, little one, but a borrowed voice must be returned when the rain ends.'

The fox roared and the animals scattered in fear. By morning he stood alone in a silent forest, and his own small voice was the only sound he wished to hear.
