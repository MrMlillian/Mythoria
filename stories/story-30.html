Home at the End of the Road

Author: Nadia Hartwell

Genre: Drama

Synopsis

After years away, a woman returns to the small town she swore she would never see again.

Chapter 1: Gravel and Rain

The road was the same, only shorter. The yellow house had faded, and the porch light, impossibly, was still on.

Her mother opened the door before she could knock and said only, 'I made too much soup.'

Inside, the kitchen clock still ran six minutes fast, and Claire realized she had missed every one of them.
