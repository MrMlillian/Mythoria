The Violin in the Attic

Author: Margot Beaumont

Genre: Drama

Synopsis

A girl finds her great-grandfather's violin and learns the song that saved her family.

Chapter 1: Dust and Strings

The strings were cracked and the wood was gray with dust. Yet when Elise lifted the bow, her fingers seemed to know where to go.

A melody rose that she had never heard, though it felt like something she'd always remembered.

Downstairs, her grandmother stopped stirring the soup and began to cry, and then to hum along.
