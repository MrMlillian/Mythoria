The Boy Who Collected Silence

Author: Theo Marchetti

Genre: Drama

Synopsis

A noisy city boy collects pockets of silence in jars to give to his sleepless grandmother.

Chapter 1: Jars of Quiet

The city never stopped talking, and neither did Leo's grandmother's worries. So Leo began catching quiet moments in empty jam jars.

He caught one in the library, one beneath the snow, and one in a church after the bells had finished ringing.

That night he opened them all around her bed, and for the first time in months, she slept like a child.
