The Midnight Library

Author: Rowan Ellis

Genre: Fantasy

Synopsis

A library opens only at midnight and lends books written about the borrower's future.

Chapter 1: Overdue

Wren found the library by accident, its door hidden between a bakery and a shuttered shoe shop. The librarian smiled as if she had been expected.

On the shelf with her name was a thin book. The first page described exactly what she was doing at that moment.

She turned the page slowly, and the librarian whispered, 'Careful. You are allowed to change the ending.'
