Seven Wishes at the Wishing Well

Author: Noor Aziz

Genre: Fantasy

Synopsis

A well grants seven wishes, but each wish takes a memory in return.

Chapter 1: The Price of Water

Samira dropped a coin into the well and wished for a warm meal. The soup appeared, and she forgot her first day at school.

She wished for shoes and lost the sound of her father's voice. She wished for a house and lost her mother's lullaby.

With one wish remaining and almost nothing left to lose, Samira leaned over the well and asked for everything back.
