Beneath the Glass Ocean

Author: Marina Volkov

Genre: Sci-Fi

Synopsis

Scientists on a frozen moon discover that the ice above a hidden sea is slowly turning transparent.

Chapter 1: The Clearing

Dr. Anwen Pike pressed her gloved palm to the ice. Yesterday it had been white as milk. Today she could see twenty meters down.

At the bottom, lights drifted in a slow, patient pattern, like a city waking up after a very long night.

'They are not hiding anymore,' she told the team over the radio. 'They are inviting us in.'
