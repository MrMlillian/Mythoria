Dust of Distant Stars

Author: Leona Castellanos

Genre: Sci-Fi

Synopsis

A scavenger finds a jar of star dust that replays memories of a vanished civilization.

Chapter 1: Starlight in a Jar

Cass sold scrap on the edge of the Orion Belt, and nothing surprised her anymore. Then she opened a jar and the cabin filled with the sound of children laughing.

Each grain held a moment: a wedding, a harvest, a goodbye in the rain.

She could have sold the jar for a fortune. Instead she sat down and listened, until the last grain had faded into silence.
