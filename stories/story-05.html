Salt and Honey

Author: Camille Dupree

Genre: Romance

Synopsis

Two rival bakers on one street discover they are each other's secret pen pal.

Chapter 1: Flour on Her Sleeve

Lucien's bakery smelled of salt and rye, and Odette's across the lane smelled of honey and cardamom. They had not spoken in six years, ever since a quarrel over a sign.

Yet each week Lucien left a letter in the old mail slot of the neighborhood tea shop and received a kind reply, signed only 'A Friend.'

On the day of the street festival, he carried his latest letter across the lane, and found Odette already holding the envelope with his handwriting.
