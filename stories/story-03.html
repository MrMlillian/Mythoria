Signal from Kepler Station

Author: Dana Okafor

Genre: Sci-Fi

Synopsis

A lonely technician receives a message from a station that was abandoned forty years ago.

Chapter 1: Static

Technician Ruiz had three months left on his shift at Kepler Station when the old radio woke up. The voice on it was calm, polite, and asked whether the supply ship had arrived yet.

He checked the logs. The station the voice described had been sealed and abandoned forty years before he was born.

He should have reported it. Instead he leaned toward the microphone and said, 'Not yet, but I'm here. Tell me what happened.'
