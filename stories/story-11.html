Nine Lives of Mr. Whiskers

Author: Jonah Pierce

Genre: Comedy

Synopsis

A cat on his ninth life tries to cram in everything he never got to do.

Chapter 1: The Bucket List

Mr. Whiskers had used eight lives already: two on a bad fall, one on a dishwasher, and five on the mailman, who never learned.

With the ninth life ahead, he made a list. Number one was to sit on the neighbor's priceless couch. Number two was to knock exactly one glass off the table, for science.

By noon he had completed the list and was napping in the sun, content, while the vacuum cleaner roared somewhere far off like a dragon he planned to ignore.
