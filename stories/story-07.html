Captain Pip and the Paper Sea

Author: Hannah Lowell

Genre: Adventure

Synopsis

A small boy sails a newspaper boat across an ocean made of old maps.

Chapter 1: Anchors Aweigh

Pip's ship was made of folded newspaper, and its sail was a postage stamp. Under his bed, the floorboards had become an endless sea of blue ink.

His crew was a grumpy cat named Biscuit and a parrot who only knew the words 'More cheese.' Together they chased the horizon.

By the third wave they found an island shaped like a question mark, and Pip knew exactly what he would ask it.
