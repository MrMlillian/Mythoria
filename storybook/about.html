About

The story behind Storybook.

Our Mission

Storybook is a small library of short stories — one chapter each — built for readers who love atmosphere, imagination, and the feeling of turning a page.

The Collection

Thirty stories spanning fantasy, sci-fi, mystery, romance, horror, adventure, and more. Each tale is self-contained, so you can start anywhere and read at your own pace.

The Project

This site was created as a final project for ITE 1. It combines semantic HTML, custom CSS, and a bit of JavaScript for filtering and theme toggling.

Credits

Designed and developed by Mark Lawrence S. Bas. All covers are original SVG artwork, and every story is written for this project.
