Letters from the Lighthouse

Author: Ingrid Solberg

Genre: Romance

Synopsis

A lighthouse keeper and a sailor exchange letters carried by gulls and tides.

Chapter 1: Message in a Bottle

Anders read the first bottle twice. The handwriting was careful, the humor gentle, and the sailor wrote as if he had known him for years.

Over the winter he wrote back each night, sealing his words with candle wax and trusting the tide to deliver them.

In spring a ship dropped anchor below the cliff, and a man stepped ashore carrying every bottle Anders had ever sent.
