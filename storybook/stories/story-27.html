Beyond the Frozen Gate

Author: Astrid Lindgaard

Genre: Adventure

Synopsis

An expedition to the arctic discovers a door in the ice that is warm to the touch.

Chapter 1: Warm Ice

The team had walked for nine days when Captain Holt found it: a doorway of dark stone standing alone in the snow.

The ice around it had melted into a perfect circle. Warm air drifted out, smelling of rain and cut grass.

Holt stepped through, and on the other side it was summer.
